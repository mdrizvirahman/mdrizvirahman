# Md Rizvi Rahman

**Pianist · Keyboardist · Music Producer · Composer** — Dhaka, Bangladesh
Also known as **Rizvi Rahman Adit** · মোঃ রিজভী রহমান

> Where words end, music begins.

Bangladeshi pianist and keyboardist with two decades on stage, and music producer and composer at the **Sound Hacker** audio production house in Dhaka.

### Bands
- Keyboardist — **Tahsan and The Band**, **Ghuddy**, **Oishee Express**
- Former keyboardist — Recall Band, BlueJeans, ShuvoJatra, Minar, Mahtim Sakib

### Selected work
- 2026 · *Domm* — "Kothay Pabo Tahare" · music arrangement
- 2025 · Clear Men "Pass Koro, Record Goro" · jingle music (Guinness World Records campaign)
- 2025 · "Kajol Chokhe Valobasha" (drama *Emon Dine Tare Bola Jay*) · piano
- 2025 · *Ek Guccho Kadam* (Closeup) · background score
- 2020 · F Minor — "Rukhe Darai Nirbhoy" · co-composer & sound engineer
